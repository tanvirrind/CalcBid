## What the SERPs show

**Paint calculator queries** — beatable. Results are DIY blogs (kulfiy, Zameen),
brand calculators (Resene, Saxon), and one strong tool+content combo
(theownerbuildernetwork.co). No dominant dedicated tool owns the query.
CalcBid's edge: a genuinely good calculator + contractor framing + quote flow.

**Roofing calculator queries** — moderately competitive. roofingcalculator.com
(exact-match domain, content-rich), webby.tools, easyprocalculator.com.
Beatable with better UX plus the FAQ depth now on the page. The Etsy
spreadsheet ranking #3 shows buyers will pay for organized estimating.

**Painting cost queries** ("interior house painting cost estimator") —
content-heavy SERPs (freeprojectestimator, thecalcs, bhumicalculator,
remodelingcalculator). These need dedicated content pages later; the paint
calculator page's FAQ is a first step, not the full answer.

**Tile calculator queries** — beatable, same pattern as paint. Results are
thin tool sites (easyunitconverter, iditect), regional players, and one
authority (Bankrate). No dominant dedicated tool. CalcBid's edge: better
calculator (tile presets, waste logic, boxes) + contractor framing + quote flow.

**Quote generator queries** — weak SERPs. YouTube videos, template.net,
invoice tools. A real interactive tool page (/quote) can outrank these.
"Painting estimate template" is dominated by thin template sites.
